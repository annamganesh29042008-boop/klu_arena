THE ARENA

ABOUT KLU ARENA

One platform for every competitor, every team and every tournament.

OUR MISSION

Competition, organized.

KLU Arena is a student-focused tournament portal designed to bring physical sports and esports together in one competitive ecosystem. Players can discover events, build teams, follow schedules, view results and chase the leaderboard.

Built as a college project with a front-end-first architecture. The current demo uses browser storage and sample tournament data; a production deployment would connect authentication, a database and live services.
