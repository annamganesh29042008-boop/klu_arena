FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers for players, teams and organizers.

How do I join a tournament?
Open the Tournaments page, choose an event, review its details and select Join Tournament. Complete the registration form to submit your team.
Can I participate in both Sports and Esports?
Yes. Sports and Esports are separate tournament categories. Participation depends on the eligibility and schedule of each individual event.
How do I create a team?
Use the Teams area to review existing squads. Create a verified roster from the Create Team page before registering.
Are the scores live?
The project includes a live-match presentation and organizer result interface for demonstration. The current version uses LocalStorage rather than a real-time server feed.
How are prizes handled?
Each tournament displays its prize pool and registration information. Organizers verify winners and record prize distribution in a production backend.
Who can organize a tournament?
Authorized organizers can use the Organizer Control Center to create events, manage registrations, publish fixtures and update results.
