PLAY FAIR

RULES & GUIDELINES

Respect the competition. Protect the community. Let skill decide the winner.

Individual tournaments may publish additional sport/game-specific rules. Always check the tournament detail page before competing.

1. Eligibility

  • Participants must satisfy the eligibility requirements shown on the tournament page.
  • Players should register with accurate student and contact information.
  • One player must not represent multiple teams in the same event unless the organizer explicitly allows it.

2. Fair Play

  • No cheating, exploits, match fixing, harassment or deliberate rule manipulation.
  • For esports, prohibited software or unauthorized assistance may result in disqualification.
  • For physical sports, participants must follow venue safety instructions and official match rules.

3. Matches & Results

  • Teams should arrive before the scheduled start time and be ready when called.
  • Organizers are responsible for publishing fixtures and recording official results.
  • Disputes should be raised through the organizer promptly with supporting evidence.

4. Prizes & Conduct

  • Prize details are published with each tournament.
  • Winners may need to complete verification before prize distribution.
  • Respect players, officials, spectators and organizers at all times.
