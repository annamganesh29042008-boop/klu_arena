PRIVACY POLICY

Simple, transparent information about this project demo.

This page describes the current college-project demo. It is not a substitute for a production legal/privacy policy.

Information we collect

The demo may collect information that you enter into forms such as a name, student ID, email, phone number, team name or message.

How the demo stores data

Some demo interactions use your browser's localStorage, including login state, tournament registration, teams, announcements and support tickets.

Third-party services

The interface loads web fonts from Google Fonts. A production version should document every external service and its data handling before launch.

Your control

You can clear the demo's local data below or through your browser's site storage controls.
